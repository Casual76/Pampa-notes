## Configuration

- User request: OpenRouter STT, use without companion, cross-device file transfer via cloud for ten minutes, targeted UX improvements.
- Confirmed: existing text synchronization stays; only audio and source file payloads expire after ten minutes.
- Implementation: at most two concurrent agents, OpenAI models selected using delegate policy. GPT-6.1 Sol for substantive work; Luna only for scoped lightweight tasks. Reasoning high or xhigh per agent.
- Review: two independent OpenAI reviewers per round, reasoning high or xhigh. At most three correction rounds after the initial review.
- One integrated acceptance phase, implemented in dependent waves; no publication or commits required by the workflow.
- Preexisting change to preserve: deleted companion/tools/__pycache__/holes.cpython-311.pyc.

## Acceptance criteria

- C1 OpenRouter: dedicated STT endpoint, transcription-only model discovery, securely stored user key, model choice, queue/worker/retry/cancellation integration. Text-only and structured responses handled without losing content; companion-only mode remains enforced. Verified by focused JVM/protocol tests.
- C2 Temporary relay: authenticated same-account access; server-clock expiry at ten minutes; expired data inaccessible; scheduled physical deletion with retry and honest documentation of scheduling latency; bounded uploads, integrity validation and account isolation. Verified against local Worker with expiry/cleanup tests.
- C3 Android transfer: upload audio and original files, discover/download on another device using synchronized identities, verify length/hash before atomic placement, survive retry/process interruption, preserve source copies and existing import/sync/archive invariants. Temporary relay must never imply durable archival or permit eviction of the last copy.
- C4 Standalone UX: OpenRouter setup and provider choice discoverable without companion; temporary transfer opt-in and statuses explain the receiving window and re-send path; no dead-end companion-only download prompts for available cloud transfers. Follow Fluid components and existing Italian/English resources.
- C5 Integration: appropriate core/app JVM tests, Android debug build, Worker typecheck and targeted protocol/regression tests pass. If device or real paid API execution is unavailable, document that limit; approval concerns verified code/protocol behavior and does not claim an unrun live deployment/API/device test.
- C6 Spending and folder policy (user added during implementation): configurable OpenRouter transcription spending budgets, cloud allow/deny rules per folder with inheritance, preflight expense estimates and actual cost reporting. Normal UI stays compact; advanced configuration remains discoverable. Re-check authorization and budget immediately before every paid chunk/retry, including import/bulk/background paths. Persist actual usage.cost and conservative unresolved-charge reservations across restart. Do not portray duration estimates as exact token-based costs or local device budgets as account-wide enforcement. Expose current key budget/remaining credit where supported and explain app versus OpenRouter guarantees. Verify hierarchy, moving folders/notes, queued policy changes, budget boundaries, retries/cancellation and unknown pricing through meaningful tests.

Review rubric: 0-3 mandatory behavior broken; 4-6 substantial omissions or risks; 7-8 requirements met with concrete minor issues; 9-10 requirements met with strong pertinent evidence. NV means not verified, NA not applicable. Approval requires all mandatory behaviors verified, no reproducible blocking/high-severity defect, both reviews available. Scores summarize evidence and do not override requirements.

## Spending-controls design added by user

- Budget settings in USD (OpenRouter billing currency), with optional per-transcription and recurring local spending limits; no invented user budget amounts. Show estimated, actual and unresolved/reserved spending separately.
- Folder policy includes inherit/allow/block with effective policy shown and optional per-folder OpenRouter model override. Global companion-only policy always wins. Resolve current hierarchy at enqueue and immediately before every upload, including notes moved while queued and all automatic/bulk paths.
- A compact preflight summary should show model, audio duration, cost estimate/range or explicitly unknown cost, and available budget; place detailed controls in an advanced section rather than repeated modal prompts.
- Use live verified model pricing metadata where its units are clear. Do not assume every pricing.prompt field means dollars per second; token-priced models need an explicitly approximate range or unknown estimate. Recheck pricing freshness; unknown/stale prices never appear as zero or free.
- Capture OpenRouter response usage.cost (actual USD) for every paid request, including chunks. Persist reservations before sending; unresolved network/cancellation/crash outcomes remain conservative reservations and are not silently refunded. Account for overlap/retries and cached chunks correctly, avoid duplicate settlement.
- App budgets constrain activity on this installation and must not be labelled as a synchronized hard account cap. Show remote key limit/limit_remaining/reset and usage where GET /api/v1/key supports it, and provide a link to configure key caps in OpenRouter without requesting/storing a management key. Do not alter provider account limits without a concrete authorized flow.
- Confirmed research: official STT docs expose usage.cost as actual USD, with duration-based and token-based pricing. Official current-key endpoint exposes limit_remaining, limit_reset and usage totals. Sources: https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/multimodal/stt and https://openrouter.ai/docs/api/api-reference/api-keys/get-current-api-key .
